No shared vault, no deposit on our side. Two wallets, one clock, and an escrow that keeps the money.

02 / How it works

Challenge. Accept. Trade. Settle.

Step 01 / Challenge

Call out a rival or post an open challenge.

  • Your stake goes into the on-chain escrow the moment you sign.
  • Pick the chain, the stake token and amount, the duel length (1 minute to 30 days) and how long the challenge stays open (5 minutes to 7 days).
  • @username or wallet: only that wallet can accept. Open link: anyone can.
  • Nobody accepts in time: cancel and your stake comes back in full.

03 / Two ways to win

Pick the game before it starts.

The mode is chosen at creation and locked with the stake.

Mode

Dollars.

Most profit in $ wins. Consistency beats one lucky shot.

score = Σ (sold or exit value − bought − gas − DEX fees)

Mode

Best trade.

Best single position multiple wins. $1 turned into $1,000 is x1000.

score = max (sold or exit value − fees) ÷ (bought + fees)
